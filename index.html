    specreel–7 field player
    reel
    hold to stop the tape · turn to scrub · flick to wind
    speed
    varispeed 0.50–2.00x, pitch follows like tape · double-tap = 1.00
    knobs
    drag up/down · double-tap resets · fx key bypasses all
    tone
    left = low-pass · right = high-pass · centre = flat
    a–b
    tap: set a · tap: set b and loop · tap: clear
    cue
    tap: drop a marker (8 per track) · hold: clear markers
    memo
    records a take from the microphone into the tape
    keys
    space play · ←/→ seek 5 s · shift+←/→ track · [ ] speed · l tape · a a–b · m cue
    formats
    mp3 wav m4a aac ogg opus flac webm — whatever this browser can decode
